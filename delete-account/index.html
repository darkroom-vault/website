Delete your Darkroom account or data

Delete some of your data

You don’t need to delete your account to remove shots. Once a vault has developed, open any shot you took, tap ⋯ and choose Remove. The file is deleted within minutes, for everyone in the vault, and highlight videos are re-made without it. To stop adding to a vault, open it and tap Leave vault. Original files are deleted automatically 7 days after a vault develops anyway.

To have other data deleted, email privacy@darkroomvault.app.

Delete your account in the app

Open Darkroom, go to your Account (the icon on the home screen), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. It happens straight away.

Delete your account without the app

Email privacy@darkroomvault.app from the email address you sign in with (or tell us which one it is), and ask us to delete your account. We’ll confirm it’s you and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, email address and sign-in details.
  • Every photo and video you took, in every vault, including any in slideshows and highlight videos (highlight videos are re-made without them).
  • Your notification settings and device token.

Vaults you host pass to another member, or are deleted if you’re the only one. Copies other members already downloaded to their own devices can’t be deleted by us. Reports you made are kept for up to 12 months for safety, then deleted.
