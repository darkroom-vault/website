Darkroom

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Darkroom is a shared camera app made by Wesley Roberts. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), anywhere in Darkroom.

What's not allowed

You must not use Darkroom to create, share, request or store:

  • child sexual abuse material of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material;
  • content that sexualises a child;
  • content or messages that groom, sextort, traffic or otherwise exploit or endanger a child.

Darkroom is only for people 16 and over (see our Terms of Use). People confirm they're 16 or older when they sign in, join a vault as a guest, or next open the app.

How we keep Darkroom safe

  • Vaults are private by default. People join with an invite link or code, and hosts can choose to approve who joins.
  • Every photo and video, in every vault whatever its settings, is checked against known child sexual abuse material before anyone can see it, using Arachnid Shield from the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (exact and visual matching, so resized or re-encoded copies still match). A match is removed straight away, the host isn't told, and we review it and report it to the authorities. A shot that can't be checked within 2 hours isn't let in.
  • In vaults with the content filter on, photos are also checked automatically for nudity and violence before anyone in the vault can see them, and videos are checked from frames taken through the clip. On the Moderate level, a video that can't be checked in time may still go in. Public events always have the filter on.
  • Anyone in a vault can report a shot. Reports go to the vault's host and to us, and we review them. We keep a reported shot until we've reviewed it, even if it's removed or its photographer deletes their account in the meantime.
  • Hosts can remove and block people from their vaults, and anyone can hide everything a person shoots.

Reporting

  • In the app: open the shot, tap ⋯ and pick a reason under Report this shot.
  • By email: write to hello@darkroomvault.app to report anything, including something you saw outside the app or an account rather than a shot.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first (000 in Australia).

What we do with reports

When we find or are told about CSAE or CSAM, we:

  • remove the content and close the accounts involved;
  • report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), the Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation (ACCCE) and other relevant authorities, as the law requires.

We're working to meet the child safety laws that apply to us, including Australia's Online Safety Act and the industry codes and standards made under it, and we'll update this page as our safeguards change.
