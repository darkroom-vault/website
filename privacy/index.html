Darkroom Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Darkroom ("we", "us") is operated by Wesley Roberts, New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Darkroom app, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions: privacy@darkroomvault.app.

The short version

  • Shots you take go into a shared vault. Nobody, including the host and you, can see them until the vault develops.
  • After it develops, people in the vault can see them (in public events: the slideshow, plus their own shots).
  • Original photos and videos are deleted 7 days after the vault develops. Only the slideshow shots and the highlight video are kept, for as long as the vault exists.
  • You can delete your account, and every shot you took, from the app at any time.
  • A shot someone has reported is kept until we've reviewed it, even if it's removed or deleted in the meantime.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.

How shots are handled

  • Before the reveal: shots are stored encrypted in transit and at rest and are not viewable by anyone through the app.
  • Known child sexual abuse material: every photo and video, in every vault, is checked against known child sexual abuse material before anyone can see it, using Arachnid Shield from the Canadian Centre for Child Protection. The file is sent to Arachnid Shield and compared with its list of known material. A match is removed straight away, kept out of sight for us to review, and reported to the authorities. A shot that can't be checked within 2 hours isn't let in.
  • Content filter: in vaults with the filter on, photos are also checked automatically for nudity and violence (using Google Cloud Vision) before anyone in the vault can see them. Videos are checked from frames taken through the clip. On the Moderate level, a video that can't be checked in time may still go in. With the filter off, there's no content filter (the check for known child sexual abuse material still runs). Hosts choose the filter level; public events always have it on.
  • Reports: anyone in a vault can report a shot they can see. Reports go to the vault's host and to us, and we review them. We keep a reported shot, and who took it, until we've reviewed it, even if it's removed or its photographer deletes their account in the meantime. We remove illegal content and report it to the authorities.
  • After the reveal: people in the vault can view and download shots as described above. Anyone with access can save copies to their own devices, which we can't control afterwards.
  • Highlight videos: we automatically make a short video from the slideshow. It's available to people in the vault.
  • Removal: the host can remove any shot, you can remove your own, and shots reported by several people are hidden automatically. Removed shots are deleted within minutes, unless they've been reported and we haven't reviewed them yet. Then they're kept, out of sight, until we have.
  • Blocking and hiding: a host can remove someone from their vault and block them from rejoining, which also removes their shots from it. You can hide a person so you don't see anything they shoot; we keep that list so it keeps working, and they aren't told.

How long we keep it

  • Original photos and videos: deleted 7 days after the vault develops.
  • Slideshow shots and highlight videos: kept while the vault exists.
  • Removed shots: deleted within minutes.
  • Reported shots: kept until we've reviewed the report, even if they'd otherwise be deleted sooner. After that, the rules above apply.
  • Your account: until you delete it. Deleting your account deletes your profile, your age confirmation and every shot you took, in every vault, except a reported shot we haven't reviewed yet, which is kept, out of sight, until we have. Vaults you host pass to another member, or are deleted if you're the only one.
  • Reports: kept for 12 months after they're made, or until we've reviewed them if that takes longer, then deleted. A report keeps a note of which shot it was about and who took it, for that time.
  • Logs: kept for up to 12 months for safety and security, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Account (the icon on the home screen) → Delete account.
  • Remove a shot you took after the reveal: open it in the slideshow → ⋯ → Remove from vault.
  • Leave a vault: your shots stay in it unless you remove them or delete your account.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Emails: turn off download reminders in the app (Account → Download reminders) or with the link at the bottom of any reminder email. Sign-in codes are always sent.
  • Access or correct your data: email privacy@darkroomvault.app.

If you're in Australia, you can complain to us first and then to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). If you're in the EU/UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and objection; contact us to use them.

International transfers

Your data is stored in Japan and processed by providers in the United States and elsewhere, as listed above. We rely on these providers' contractual protections for international transfers.

Children

Darkroom is only for people 16 and over, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted by strict permissions in our database, and shots are not viewable by anyone until a vault develops. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at privacy@darkroomvault.app if you find a problem.

Changes

We'll update this policy as Darkroom changes and show the new effective date above. For significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
